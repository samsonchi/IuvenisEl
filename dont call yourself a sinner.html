Don't call yourself a sinner

It's pride to call yourself a sinner when God said you're righteous

by Diary of a Jesus Girl

I was supposed to serve under the National Youth Service Scheme in 2015. My results were completely processed, I had also received my certificate, meaning I was totally cleared from the University of Calabar and eligible for the scheme. I scheduled a day to go register online but within that period as I prayed, I lost my peace. I don't know how but I knew deep within me that God wanted me to wait. That year, 2015, I stayed indoors ninety percent of the time. With school in session, I was alone at home most of the day. I spent it reading the Bible in different translations. I had questions. Deep questions. It was still the period the religion in me was struggling with the voice of the Holyspirit. I remembered how Abba would tug at my heart to pray and the first thing I would search for is a scarf with which to cover my head. This is just a mini example. I was confused. Can I pray anywhere and anytime? Will God hear me even if I forgot a scarf? Little as it seems , I felt guilty each time I would desire to pray and found no scarf. "Father I come before you as a sinner" felt too heavy to say because i had heard Abba whisper "It's pride to call yourself a sinner when I said you're righteous". But I was taught to pray this all my life. 2015 was the year I began to personally and deeply study God's Word. It was worth the time! I would gladly delay one year before service. : You see, on the day you were born again, a lot of things changed. You were placed in Christ and his death became yours. You lost your sin. Your old self was done away with. You received his peace, righteousness, and very life. But two things did not change. First, your physical body did not change. Although you personally were saved, your earthsuit is still getting older one year at a time. We are still waiting eagerly for “the redemption of our bodies” (Rom 8:23). Second, beyond repenting and deciding to trust Jesus with your life, your way of thinking probably did not change. If you liked chocolate and drove badly before you were saved, then you probably liked chocolate and drove badly after you were saved. God’s intention is for you to reign in this life (Rom 5:17). His plan is for you to be the head and not the tail, to be on top of your circumstances and not under them. However, this will not be your experience until you appreciate what God has done in your past. Every believer knows what happened to Jesus on the cross, but not every believer knows what happened to them. Because of this they’re striving to become someone they already are and they’re fighting to obtain something they already have. They pray like beggars instead of commanders and they see themselves as servants instead of sons. The instant you were born again, God did an amazing work in your life. He gave you a glorious new past and a completely new life. He changed just about everything about you. But one thing God left unchanged was your mind, your way of thinking.
